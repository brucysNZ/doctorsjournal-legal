🔑 Signing in (both phones)
On the sign-in screen you have three easy options — pick any:
  1. Continue with Google — tap it, choose your Google account, done. (Easiest — most people use this.)
  2. Continue with Apple — iPhone only. Use Face ID / Touch ID to sign in without giving Apple your email if you prefer.
  3. Email & password — enter your email and a password to create your login. Use this if you'd rather not use Google or Apple.
Whichever you pick, your medical journal is encrypted on your phone and backed up to your own Google Drive or iCloud — never to Bruce.
