🔑 Signing in (both phones)
On the sign-in screen you have three easy options — pick any:
  1. Continue with Google — tap it, choose your Google account, done. (Easiest — most people use this.)
  2. Continue with Apple — iPhone only. Use Face ID / Touch ID to sign in without giving Apple your email if you prefer.
  3. Email & password — enter your email and a password to create your login. Use this if you'd rather not use Google or Apple.
Whichever you pick, your medical journal is encrypted on your phone and backed up to your own Google Drive or iCloud — never to Bruce.

🌿 Iwi & Hapū (Organisation-Subsidised)

Iwi or hapū pay part of the subscription for their registered whānau. Bypasses Apple's 30% cut — more of every dollar stays with the app + the whānau member gets a discount.

Provider-Subsidy Model

Iwi/hapū sets a subsidy % (0–100). Whānau member pays the remainder via web portal — no Apple IAP.
Example — Ngāti Pikiao Rūnanga at 50% (pilot partner)
Whānau member pays
NZ$5 / month
Ngāti Pikiao contributes
NZ$5 / month
Total to Doctor's Journal (after Stripe fees ~2.9%)
NZ$9.70 / month
Why this saves everyone money: Apple IAP takes ~30% (NZ$3 out of every NZ$10). Stripe takes ~3% (NZ$0.30). At 100 whānau members, that's NZ$270/month saved vs Apple. At 1,000 members, NZ$2,700/month saved — money that can go back into further subsidising members OR extending coverage.
  • Iwi/hapū picks the subsidy percentage — 25%, 50%, 100% (fully free for whānau), or custom
  • Registered on a roster (NHI + name + DOB) — one entitlement per person, no misuse
  • Whānau sign up at a dedicated web portal, no Apple/Google account needed for billing
  • Iwi/hapū receives a single monthly invoice for the aggregated subsidy amount
  • Full app features unlocked — identical to individual Premium
  • Members keep their subscription if they leave the iwi (they take over payment)
Pilot: Ngāti Pikiao Rūnanga (pikiaorunanga.org.nz) — first partner. Contact Bruce to onboard your iwi/hapū.
