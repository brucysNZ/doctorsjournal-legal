Doctor's Journal

Your doctor visits, medications, documents and test results, together in one private journal on your phone.

What it does

  • Record a doctor's appointment and get a plain-English summary of what was said.
  • Keep a list of your medications, with reminders to take them.
  • Photograph or upload letters, prescriptions and test results, and have them explained simply.
  • See your appointments and follow-ups in a calendar, with reminders.
  • Keep emergency information and contacts ready when you need them.
  • Look after family members with their own separate profiles.

Your data

Your journal is stored on your phone. Recordings and documents are sent securely for AI processing only to create your summaries, and are not kept for training. Backups are encrypted and saved to your own iCloud (iPhone) or Google Drive (Android) account. If you sign in with Google, the app asks for access to Google Drive only to save and restore your own encrypted backup.
